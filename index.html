    Buying guide

    Amazon

    Best for branded items like controllers and licensed figures. Look for "Sold by" and "Fulfilled by Amazon", read the one-star reviews first and check the return window.

    Flipkart

    Strong for fashion, sneakers and electronics, with frequent sale days. Filter by "Flipkart Assured" and compare the final price after bank offers.

    Meesho

    Known for low prices on fashion, accessories and everyday beauty items. Quality varies by seller, so check seller rating, real customer photos and the return policy before ordering.

    Spotting fakes

    Figures, controllers and skincare are copied a lot. Be careful with prices far below the usual range, blurry photos, missing brand logos and sellers with very few ratings.

    Skincare safety

    Patch test any new product on a small area first and stop if your skin reacts. For skin problems or allergies, ask a dermatologist before trying something trending.
